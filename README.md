# MedWaste AI

MedWaste AI is a digital medical waste management platform designed to connect hospitals, waste collectors, treatment facilities and recycling companies. The platform manages medical waste from reporting and classification through pickup, transportation, treatment/recycling, final verification and analytics.

## Core Goals
- Digitize medical waste lifecycle management
- Improve traceability and chain of custody
- Support recycling of eligible waste
- Improve coordination between hospitals and processing organizations
- Provide operational and environmental analytics
- Support future local AI capabilities without requiring paid external APIs

## Planned Technology
- Next.js + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS
- FastAPI + Python
- PostgreSQL
- SQLAlchemy + Alembic
- Local AI in later development phases

## Main User Roles
Hospital | Waste Collector | Treatment Facility | Recycling Company | Administrator

## Development Approach
The project will be implemented incrementally through defined development phases. Each phase is tested before the next phase begins.

## Current Status
Week 1 — Initiation & SRS

## Important Note
MedWaste AI is an academic/startup prototype. AI recommendations are not a substitute for applicable medical, environmental, safety, or regulatory requirements.
